Born in Santa Tereza, Belo Horizonte, Daniel Gosling grew up inside the musical landscape that gave birth to Clube da Esquina.

Family, neighbourhood, friendship and music were never separate things.

After studying at Bituca University of Popular Music and years inside Belo Horizonte's music scene, that language travelled with him to Australia.

It still does.
