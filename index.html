01 / ORIGIN

Before I played it,
I already
knew the sound.

I grew up in Santa Tereza, Belo Horizonte — the neighbourhood where Clube da Esquina took shape.

Music was everywhere: family gatherings, guitars, voices, records, people dropping by. My grandmother knew Maricota Borges, mother of Lô and Márcio. The songs of Milton, Lô, Beto and Toninho never felt distant.

They sounded like home.

SANTÊ

02 / RECORDING

2022 · original composition

Santê

feat. Toninho Horta · Esdra Ferreira “Neném” · Enéias Xavier

A song for the streets where it all began.

Santê is my memory of Santa Tereza in music.

Recording it with Toninho Horta brought the story full circle: one of the musicians who shaped the sound of that place playing on a song written about it.

03 / THE ROAD
Some things are
learned in school.
Others, by being there.

After five years in Porto Alegre, I returned to Minas and joined Bituca — Universidade de Música Popular in 2014, studying with Ian Guest, Gilvan de Oliveira, Lincoln Cheib and Enéias Xavier.

But the real education was larger than any classroom: years inside Belo Horizonte’s music scene, surrounded by musicians, friendships and the people behind the records I had grown up hearing.

04 / INTERPRETATION

2025 · composition by Edelson Pantera

Faial

Deangelo Silva · Felipe Continentino · Igor Gosling

Four musicians. One beautiful piece. No need to play it safe.

Faial was written by Edelson Pantera.

I recorded it with musicians whose voices I love: Deangelo Silva on piano, Felipe Continentino on drums and my brother Igor Gosling on bass. The idea was simple — give the composition room and let everybody sound like themselves.

05 / COLLABORATION
CANÇÃO DA ESTRADA ABERTA

Daniel Gosling · Mário Wamser · lyrics by Murilo Antunes

It started with a few chords and nowhere to go.

I sent them to Mário Wamser. He found a melody and opened another door. Then Murilo Antunes — one of the lyricists behind Clube da Esquina — heard the piece and wrote the words.

Inspired by Walt Whitman’s Song of the Open Road, he gave it a name: Canção da Estrada Aberta.

The road appeared after the music had already started walking.

06 / MINAS → AUSTRALIA

Same language.
New landscape.

Today I live in Adelaide, fourteen thousand kilometres from Belo Horizonte.

In 2023, at Nexus Arts during Adelaide Fringe, I played a concert built around the music of Clube da Esquina and Minas Gerais.

The point was never nostalgia. It was seeing what happens when the music you carry finds a new room.

08 / BIO

Daniel Gosling

Brazilian guitarist and composer from Santa Tereza, Belo Horizonte, now based in Adelaide, Australia.

His music carries the harmonic language of Minas Gerais into original compositions and collaborations, including Santê with Toninho Horta and Canção da Estrada Aberta with Mário Wamser and Murilo Antunes.
